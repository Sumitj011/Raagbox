RaagBox

Desi radio, genre by genre. Pick a genre, press play, no signup.

RaagBox turns YouTube playlists into radio stations: Bollywood, Punjabi, Ghazal and your own mixes. It has synced lyrics, a sleep timer, theater mode, and optional Google login to sync your stations across devices.

Features
Stations backed by YouTube playlists, with shuffle
All Shuffle: hops across all your stations
Lyrics from lrclib.net, with manual sync adjustment
Favorites and recently played
Sleep timer and theater mode
Manage panel to edit stations, plus export/import code
Optional Google login with cloud sync (Firebase)
Installable on your phone as a PWA or APK
Offline

The app opens offline (page, wallpaper, icons, saved stations), but music and lyrics need internet. Songs stream from YouTube and lyrics come from lrclib.net.

Install on your phone

On Android Chrome, open the site and choose Install app.

To build an APK, open pwabuilder.com, enter your site URL and pick Android, then APK. Keep the signing key it gives you, because you need it for updates.

Note

Background playback is limited by the YouTube embed. Audio often stops when the screen locks or you switch apps.
